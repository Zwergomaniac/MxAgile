# Mercedes-Benz AG - Glossary

This document contains standard definitions for terms used across MB AG projects.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| FIN | Fahrzeug-Identifizierungsnummer (Vehicle Identification Number) |
| Baureihe | A series of vehicles (e.g., C-Class, S-Class). |
| NTB | "Nach Termin und Bedarf" (According to schedule and need). |
